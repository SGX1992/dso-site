FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Contact Us
What is the Knowledge & Innovation Ecosystem?
Everything in Dubai Silicon Oasis that helps a technology company beyond its licence: industry clusters, a startup campus, a venture fund, a university, testbeds and accelerator programmes.
Which industries does Dubai Silicon Oasis focus on?
Eleven industry clusters, from AI, robotics and future mobility to 3D printing, cybersecurity and Web3. See the emerging clusters and the strategic industries.
Can I test a product in the city before launch?
Yes. Delivery robots, drone delivery and autonomous vehicles have all been trialled here with the relevant regulators. See Testbed & Piloting Facilities.
Is there funding for startups?
Oraseya Capital, the venture capital arm of Dubai Integrated Economic Zones, is based in the zone and invests from pre-seed to Series B. Its Sandbox accelerator takes pre-seed and seed founders.
Are there universities in the city?
Yes. RIT Dubai, the Dubai campus of Rochester Institute of Technology, has been here since 2008, and more than 15 learning and R&D institutions work in the city.
How do I become part of a cluster?
When you set up in the zone you join your industry cluster, its events and the Member Portal. The setup planner is the quickest way to start.
