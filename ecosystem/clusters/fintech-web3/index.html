Overview

Where digital finance meets distributed technology

Web3 is one of the 11 industry clusters of Dubai Silicon Oasis and one of the six that District IO is planned around. Telr, the payment gateway, is among the companies based in the zone.

The zone gives these companies a licence, a workplace and a network. Activities that are regulated, such as payment services or virtual assets, also need approval from the relevant UAE authority.

Why DSO

Four reasons fintech companies choose DSO

Venture capital in the zone

Oraseya Capital invests from pre-seed to Series B and is based in Dubai Silicon Oasis.

Hosting close by

Gulf Data Hub operates in the zone and AI-ready data centres are being developed with VOLT UAE.

Free zone framework

Full foreign ownership and Qualifying Free Zone status for UAE corporate tax.

A base in Dubai

A free zone company can serve customers across the region and internationally.

Operational foundation

Infrastructure for financial platforms

Financial products run on uptime and trust. Data centre capacity sits inside the zone, offices range from a desk at Dtec to a floor at Dubai Digital Park, and one authority issues the licence, the lease and the visas.

  • One authority for licence and visas

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Contact Us
What activities fit this cluster?
Digital payments, blockchain platforms, decentralised applications, tokenisation and related financial technology, subject to licensing approval.
Can blockchain and Web3 companies set up here?
Yes. Web3 is one of the 11 industry clusters of Dubai Silicon Oasis.
Does a free zone licence cover regulated financial services?
Not on its own. Activities such as payment services or virtual-asset services also need approval from the relevant UAE regulator. Your advisor tells you which apply to your activity.
Is funding available?
Oraseya Capital, the venture capital arm of DIEZ, invests from pre-seed to Series B with tickets of up to USD 6 million. Investment decisions are made by the fund.
What are the ownership and tax positions?
Companies can be fully foreign-owned. Dubai Silicon Oasis is a Qualifying Free Zone under the UAE Corporate Tax Law, and qualifying income may be taxed at 0%, subject to the relevant decisions. This is general information, not tax advice.
Can I serve international markets from here?
Yes. A free zone company can trade internationally and provide services across the region.
