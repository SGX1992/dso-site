Expand or transfer your business to Dubai Silicon Oasis

Three ways to bring an existing company into the zone: open a branch, form a subsidiary or move the company itself.

Guide5 min readUpdated November 2026
In short
  • A branch has no separate legal personality and no share capital
  • A subsidiary is a Free Zone Company with its own legal personality
  • A transfer moves the company and keeps its history
  • A mainland company can work in parts of the zone under a Business Operation Permit

Second presence, or a move

Start with one decision. Expanding leaves your existing company where it is and adds a presence in Dubai Silicon Oasis. Transferring moves the company’s registration into the free zone. The three options below follow from that.

Option 1: open a branch

A branch is an extension of a company incorporated elsewhere. It has no separate legal personality and no share capital, and the parent company answers for it. This suits established companies that want a UAE presence on the parent’s balance sheet.

Option 2: form a subsidiary

A Free Zone Company (FZCO) is a limited liability company with its own legal personality. Its shareholders can be individuals or companies, so the parent can hold the shares alone or with partners. Groups choose this when the UAE business should stand on its own.

Option 3: transfer the company

The regulations provide for transfer, also called continuation, into or out of the free zone. The company continues as the same legal entity, with its contracts and its history. Whether a transfer is possible also depends on the law of the place the company is leaving, so take advice in both jurisdictions before you start.

Already licensed on the Dubai mainland

In the privately developed areas of the zone, known as the Administrative Zone, a company with a Dubai mainland licence can operate under a Business Operation Permit. The permit is issued on the basis of the mainland licence and involves no share capital.

Space for an established team

An existing company usually arrives with people. Plug-and-play offices start at 160 sq ft, TechnoHub 2 takes teams of 2 to 500, and Dubai Digital Park offers units of up to 10,000 sq m. See Choose Your Office or Facility.

What to prepare

The checklist of required documents is on the Legislation & Regulations page. In general, expect to provide:

  • The company documents of the parent or of the company being transferred
  • A decision of the owners to open the branch, form the subsidiary or transfer
  • Passport copies of the managers
  • A description of the activity in the UAE

Tax and legal consequences differ by route and by country of origin. This guide is general information, not legal or tax advice.
