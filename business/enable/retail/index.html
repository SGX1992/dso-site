FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Contact Us
Which retail activities are permitted?
Shops, food and beverage and services, each subject to approval of the activity. Your advisor confirms the licence for your concept.
Where are the retail locations?
In the neighbourhood shopping centres and at street level in Dubai Digital Park. The leasing team tells you which units are open.
Who are the customers?
A community of more than 100,000 residents and employees, students of RIT Dubai and guests of the hotels in the city.
Is 100% foreign ownership allowed for retail businesses?
Yes. A company licensed in Dubai Silicon Oasis can be fully foreign-owned.
Can I operate with a mainland licence?
In the privately developed areas of the zone, a company with a Dubai mainland licence can operate under a Business Operation Permit.
What is the corporate tax position?
Dubai Silicon Oasis is a Qualifying Free Zone under the UAE Corporate Tax Law. Qualifying income may be taxed at 0%, subject to the relevant Cabinet and Ministerial decisions. This page is general information, not tax advice.
