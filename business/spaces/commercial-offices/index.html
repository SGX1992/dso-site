Where companies build and scale

Commercial offices in Dubai Silicon Oasis come in three settings: the Headquarters at the centre of the business district, the TechnoHub campuses and the smart district of Dubai Digital Park.

All of them are let by the same landlord, so taking more space is a change to your lease, not a relocation.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Contact Us
What types of office are available?
Plug-and-play offices from 160 sq ft at the DSO Headquarters, floors at TechnoHub 2 and TechnoHub 5, and units from 50 sq m in the eight office buildings of Dubai Digital Park.
Are the offices fully equipped?
Plug-and-play offices are ready to work in, with connectivity and air conditioning in place. Ask the team whether a larger floor is offered fitted or as shell-and-core.
Which services are in the district?
Banks, restaurants and a post office at the Headquarters, shops and cafés at Dubai Digital Park, and hotels, schools and a hospital in the wider city.
Can we expand later?
Yes. Companies move from an office to a floor, or from a floor to a building, without changing their licence.
How many visas come with an office?
The visa allowance is based on the space you take. Your advisor confirms the number with the lease.
