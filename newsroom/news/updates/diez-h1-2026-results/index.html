First half of 2026: new registrations at Dtec up 57%

DIEZ reports growth across its three zones. At Dtec, AI-focused companies almost doubled.

Results17 Aug 20261 min read

Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority has published its results for the first half of 2026: AED 246 billion in trade, 96% occupancy, 13% more companies and a 24% larger workforce across Dubai Airport Freezone, Dubai Silicon Oasis and Dubai CommerCity.

At Dubai Silicon Oasis

  • New company registrations at Dtec rose 57%
  • AI-focused companies at Dtec rose 95%
  • The eighth Sandbox cohort drew 771 applications for 16 places
