Talks and demonstrations on emerging technology

IO Labs is the event series of District IO. Founders, engineers, investors and industry leaders meet in Dubai Silicon Oasis through the year.

Register interest About District IO
IO Labs

One day, one subject

Each IO Labs event brings innovators, researchers and industry leaders together for a day of talks and working sessions on a technology that is changing markets.

Past events covered agentic AI, robotics and applications of intelligent systems in the field.

  • Complimentary registration
  • Limited-capacity sessions
  • Held in Dubai Silicon Oasis
Why attend

What a day at IO Labs includes

Speakers who build

People working on AI systems, robotics and the technologies that follow.

Emerging technologies

How autonomous AI systems and similar technologies are used in practice.

Applied work

Intelligent systems that are already deployed, shown by the teams behind them.

Live demonstrations

See applications and new systems in operation.

The ecosystem in one place

Founders, investors and company leaders from across the technology sector.

Time to meet

Meet innovators and decision-makers between the sessions.

Venue

Radisson RED, Dubai Silicon Oasis

IO Labs takes place at Radisson RED in Dubai Silicon Oasis, a hotel with event space in the middle of the district.
