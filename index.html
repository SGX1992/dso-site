FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Contact Us
Can multinational companies establish regional headquarters in DSO?
Yes. Dubai Silicon Oasis provides a structured free zone framework suitable for multinational corporations establishing regional headquarters in the Middle East. Enterprises benefit from scalable commercial infrastructure and a regulated business environment designed for long-term operations.
What are the tax benefits for enterprise-level companies?
Qualifying free zone companies benefit from a 0% corporate tax rate on qualifying income under the UAE corporate tax regime, no personal income tax, customs duty exemptions within the zone and full repatriation of capital and profits.
Is 100% foreign ownership permitted?
Yes. Every company licensed in Dubai Silicon Oasis can be fully foreign-owned. No local sponsor or service agent is required.
What commercial spaces are available for large teams?
DSO offers fitted and shell-and-core offices in TechnoHub and Silicon Park, boutique office buildings, light industrial units, land plots and built-to-suit developments. Teams can start in Dtec coworking and scale into a dedicated headquarters without leaving the city.
Can enterprises collaborate with startups within DSO?
Absolutely. Dtec hosts more than a thousand startups, Oraseya Capital invests from within the zone and the industry clusters run regular programmes, pilots and testbeds, including drone, robotics and autonomous mobility trials on DSO's own streets.
What regulatory approvals are required to establish an enterprise in DSO?
Company registration and licensing are handled by the Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority (DIEZ) through a single-window process. Depending on the activity, sector-specific approvals are coordinated by the DSO business setup team.
