The campus

Purpose-built for headquarters and R&D

TechnoHub 4 is a purpose-built corporate campus in Dubai Silicon Oasis with office space for global technology companies and high-impact enterprises.

The building has four floors with modular floorplates and connected wings, so headquarters teams, engineering units and research functions can share one layout. It has seven units, reserved for companies that fit the innovation mandate of Dubai Silicon Oasis.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Contact Us
What types of companies are eligible for TechnoHub 4?
Multinational corporations and high-impact technology companies in fields such as AI, robotics, mobility, cybersecurity, semiconductors and enterprise software. Admission is based on strategic fit with the innovation mandate of Dubai Silicon Oasis and on what a company adds to the ecosystem.
Can companies scale their space over time?
Yes. The modular floorplates of the G+3 building let a team expand into adjacent wings, subject to availability. A company that outgrows the building can move to a headquarters building, light industrial units or a built-to-suit plot elsewhere in Dubai Silicon Oasis.
What is the parking eligibility policy?
Tenants are eligible for one parking space per 50 sq m of leased area. Additional spaces can be rented by the year on request, subject to availability. There is parking for visitors as well.
What support does DSO provide?
Licensing and regulatory approvals, visa processing, fit-out coordination, utilities activation and access to all free zone services from the start of operations. After onboarding, companies take part in the industry clusters, events and collaboration of the ecosystem.
Are companies in TechnoHub 4 eligible for the UAE’s upcoming RDI tax incentive?
Eligibility will depend on the final federal legislation, the scope of a company’s qualifying R&D activities and the criteria set by the UAE authorities. Once the framework is confirmed, DSO will help eligible companies assess how they qualify. This is general information, not tax advice.
Why should companies consider TechnoHub 4?
It combines a low-rise Grade-A building, modular floorplates and a location inside the innovation district of Dubai Silicon Oasis, with free zone setup and a curated group of global businesses and technology companies as neighbours.
